Euclid Telescope

A telescope built by ESA and NASA to detect Dark Matter The target of the Euclid Telescope is understand dark energy and dark matter by measuring the expansion of the universe.
The mission is named after the ancient Greek mathematician Euclid. Euclid was launched on 1 July 2023 from Cape Canaveral in Florida using SpaceX's Falcon 9. The first images from Euclid was received and release to the public 7 November 2023.
It current orbits the Sun at the Lagrange Point 2 (L2). Euclid's detection wavelength is at Visible or Near-Infrared, it carries the VIS (Visible imager) and NISP (Near-Infrared Spectrometer) instruments.
It's goal is to map 1.5 billion Galaxies to study Dark matter and over a peroid of 6 years (Mission Duration)
