Euclid Telescope

The target of the Euclid Telescope is understand dark energy and dark matter by measuring the expansion of the universe.
The mission is named after the ancient Greek mathematician Euclid. Euclid was launched on 1 July 2023 from Cape Canaveral in Florida. The first images from Euclid is on 7 November 2023,

ESA/Euclid/Euclid Consortium/NASA, image processing by J.-C. Cuillandre (CEA Paris-Saclay), G. Anselmi

Euclid's First Image

ESA/Euclid/Euclid Consortium/NASA, image processing by the Euclid Science Ground Segment and M. Schirmer (MPIA)

This is one of Euclid's Recent Images (On 22, December 2025) This galaxy is NGC 646, and it's neighbour PGC 6014 (On the left). They look like neighbours, but they are 4 million years apart
