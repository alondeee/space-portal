Lepus

Lepus, the hare, is a constellation located just south of Orion, often depicted being hunted by him.

    • Lepus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 51st largest Constellation
  • Area: 290 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Arneb (α Leporis) - magnitude 2.58
  • Best Visible: December - February
  • Mythology: Represents a hare being pursued by Orion, the hunter, and his dogs Canis Major and Canis Minor.
  • Contains Hind's Crimson Star, one of the reddest stars known.
  • Neighbours: Orion, Monoceros, Canis Major, Columba, Caelum, Eridanus
