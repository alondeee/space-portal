    • Apus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 67th largest Constellation
  • Area: 206 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: α Apodis (mag 3.8)
  • Best Visible: July - August
  • Meaning: "No Feet" in Greek, referring to the Bird of Paradise which was once believed to have no feet
  • Introduced: By Pieter Dirkszoon Keyser and Frederick de Houtman in the 16th century
  • Neighbours: Ara, Triangulum Australe, Circinus, Musca, Chamaeleon, Octans, Pavo
  • Location: Far southern sky, not visible from Europe
