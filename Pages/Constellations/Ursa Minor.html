Ursa Minor

Ursa Minor, the Little Bear, is famous for containing the current northern pole star, Polaris.

Ursa Minor

  • Major Stars (Little Dipper):
  • 1. Polaris (α Ursae Minoris) - The North Star
  • 2. Kochab (β Ursae Minoris)
  • 3. Pherkad (γ Ursae Minoris)
  • 4. ε Ursae Minoris
  • 5. ζ Ursae Minoris (Akhfa al Farkadain)
  • 6. η Ursae Minoris (Anwar al Farkadain)
  • 7. δ Ursae Minoris (Yildun)
  • 8. λ Ursae Minoris
  • 9. π¹ Ursae Minoris
  • 10. π² Ursae Minoris

    • Notable Stars:

  • Polaris - a Cepheid variable star, crucial for navigation
  • Kochab and Pherkad are known as the "Guardians of the Pole"

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • NGC 6217 - Barred Spiral Galaxy
  • NGC 5832 - Barred Spiral Galaxy
  • NGC 3172 (Polarissima Borealis) - Galaxy closest to the north celestial pole
  • Little Dumbbell Nebula (M76) - often listed in Perseus, but sometimes associated with bordering Ursa Minor

    • Ursa Minor - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 56th largest Constellation
  • Area: 256 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Polaris (α Ursae Minoris) - magnitude 1.98
  • Best Visible: All year (circumpolar for northern hemisphere)
  • Mythology: Represents Arcas, son of Callisto (Ursa Major), who was transformed into a bear and placed in the sky by Zeus.
  • Polaris is currently the northern pole star, but this changes over millennia due to precession.
  • Neighbours: Draco, Camelopardalis, Cepheus
