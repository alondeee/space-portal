Libra

Libra, the scales, is a zodiacal constellation and the only one named after an inanimate object.

    • Libra - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 29th largest Constellation
  • Area: 538 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Zubeneschamali (β Librae) - magnitude 2.61
  • Best Visible: April - July
  • Mythology: Represents the scales of justice held by the goddess Astraea (associated with Virgo). In ancient times, its stars were considered part of Scorpius's claws.
  • Zubenelgenubi (α Librae) is actually a binary star system and its name means "the southern claw" in Arabic, reflecting its past association with Scorpius.
  • Neighbours: Serpens Caput, Virgo, Hydra, Centaurus, Lupus, Scorpius, Ophiuchus
