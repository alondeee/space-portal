Mensa

A faint southern constellation named after Table Mountain in South Africa

Mensa

  • Major Stars:
  • 1. Alpha Mensae - Yellow-white dwarf, brightest star
  • 2. Gamma Mensae - Binary star system
  • 3. Beta Mensae - Yellow-white giant
  • 4. Eta Mensae - Orange giant
  • 5. Zeta Mensae - Binary star
  • 6. Iota Mensae - White main sequence
  • 7. Mu Mensae - Binary star
  • 8. Delta Mensae - Yellow-white dwarf
  • 9. Theta Mensae - Red giant
  • 10. Lambda Mensae - White main sequence

    • Notable Stars:

  • Pi Mensae - Yellow dwarf with exoplanet (super-Earth)
  • HD 38283 - Star with exoplanet discovered in 2011

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • Large Magellanic Cloud (partially) - Dwarf galaxy, Milky Way satellite
  • NGC 1841 - Globular cluster in LMC
  • DEM L316 - Supernova remnants in LMC
  • PGC 12695 - Spiral galaxy
  • ESO 56-115 - Emission nebula in LMC
  • Mensa contains part of the LMC's southern region

    • Mensa - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 75th largest Constellation
  • Area: 153 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpha Mensae (magnitude 5.09)
  • Best Visible: January - February (from southern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Named by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille as Mons Mensae after Table Mountain (Mensa Table) in South Africa.
  • Contains part of the Large Magellanic Cloud, one of our closest galactic neighbors.
  • Neighbours: Chamaeleon, Volans, Dorado, Hydrus, Octans
