Cancer

The Crab - Faintest zodiac constellation, home to the Beehive Cluster

Cancer - Major Stars

  • 1. Al Tarf (β Cancri) - Brightest star in Cancer
  • 2. Acubens (α Cancri) - Binary star system
  • 3. Asellus Borealis (γ Cancri)
  • 4. Asellus Australis (δ Cancri)
  • 5. ζ Cancri - Multiple star system
  • 6. ι Cancri
  • 7. θ Cancri
  • 8. η Cancri
  • 9. μ Cancri
  • 10. 55 Cancri - Known exoplanet system

Notable Stars & Exoplanets

  • 55 Cancri - Has 5 exoplanets (Copernicus system)
  • HD 73534 - Star with a Jupiter-like planet

Deep Sky Objects

  • M44 (Praesepe/Beehive Cluster) - Open cluster visible to naked eye
  • M67 - One of the oldest open clusters
  • NGC 2775 - Spiral galaxy
  • NGC 2535 & NGC 2536 - Interacting galaxies

Cancer - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 31st largest constellation
  • Area: 506 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Al Tarf (β Cancri)
  • Best Visible: Winter - Spring (January - May)
  • Mythology: Represents the crab sent by Hera to distract Hercules
  • Contains the Beehive Cluster (M44), known since ancient times
  • Neighbours: Leo, Hydra, Lynx, Gemini, Canis Minor
