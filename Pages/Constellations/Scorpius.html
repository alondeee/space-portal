Scorpius

Scorpius, the scorpion, is a prominent zodiacal constellation located near the center of the Milky Way.

Scorpius

  • Major Stars:
  • 1. Antares (α Scorpii)
  • 2. Shaula (λ Scorpii)
  • 3. Sargas (θ Scorpii)
  • 4. δ Scorpii (Dschubba)
  • 5. ε Scorpii
  • 6. κ Scorpii
  • 7. β Scorpii (Acrab)
  • 8. υ Scorpii (Lesath)
  • 9. τ Scorpii
  • 10. π Scorpii

    • Notable Stars:

  • Antares - red supergiant, one of the largest and brightest stars visible to the naked eye
  • ρ Ophiuchi - part of the extensive Rho Ophiuchi cloud complex

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • M4 - Globular Cluster (one of the closest to Earth)
  • M6 (Butterfly Cluster) - Open Cluster
  • M7 (Ptolemy's Cluster) - Open Cluster
  • M80 - Globular Cluster
  • NGC 6302 (Butterfly Nebula) - Planetary Nebula
  • NGC 6334 (Cat's Paw Nebula) - Emission Nebula
  • NGC 6357 (Lobster Nebula) - Emission Nebula

    • Scorpius - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 33rd largest Constellation
  • Area: 497 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Antares (α Scorpii) - 15th brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: May - August
  • Mythology: Represents the scorpion that killed the hunter Orion. According to myth, they were placed on opposite sides of the sky so they never rise or set together.
  • Its location in the Milky Way makes it rich in star clusters and nebulae.
  • Neighbours: Sagittarius, Ophiuchus, Libra, Lupus, Norma, Ara, Corona Australis
