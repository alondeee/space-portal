    • Norma - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 74th largest Constellation
  • Area: 165 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Gamma Normae (magnitude 4.00)
  • Best Visible: July (from southern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Created by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille as Norma et Regula, representing a carpenter's square and level.
  • Contains part of the Great Attractor region, including the Norma Cluster (Abell 3627).
  • Neighbours: Scorpius, Lupus, Circinus, Triangulum Australe, Ara
