Canis Major

The Greater Dog - Contains Sirius, the brightest star in the night sky

  • 4. Aludra (η Canis Majoris) - Blue-white supergiant
  • 5. Mirzam (β Canis Majoris) - Blue giant
  • 6. Muliphein (γ Canis Majoris)
  • 7. Furud (ζ Canis Majoris)

Canis Major - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 43rd largest constellation
  • Area: 380 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Sirius (α Canis Majoris) - Brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: Winter (December - March)
  • Mythology: Represents the larger of Orion's two hunting dogs
  • Contains the "Dog Star" Sirius, important in ancient Egyptian calendar
  • Neighbours: Monoceros, Lepus, Columba, Puppis
