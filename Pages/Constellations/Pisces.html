    • Pisces — Foundational Data

  • Rank: 14th largest constellation
  • Area: 889 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: η Psc (Alpherg — mag 3.62)
  • Best Visible: September — November
  • Mythology: Represents Aphrodite and Eros transformed into fish to escape Typhon; tied by a cord.
  • Contains the Vernal Equinox (March equinox point).
  • Neighbours: Andromeda, Pegasus, Aquarius, Cetus, Aries, Triangulum
