    • Lynx - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 28th largest Constellation
  • Area: 545 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpha Lyncis (magnitude 3.14)
  • Best Visible: February - March (from northern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Created by Johannes Hevelius in the 17th century. Named for the lynx because it's so faint that one needs "lynx-like" eyes to see it.
  • Contains NGC 2419, one of the most distant globular clusters in the Milky Way halo, possibly of extragalactic origin.
  • NGC 2770 has hosted multiple supernovae, earning it the nickname "Supernova Factory."
  • Neighbours: Ursa Major, Camelopardalis, Auriga, Gemini, Cancer, Leo Minor

    • Notable Exoplanets in Lynx

  • XO-2 System: Binary star system where both stars host multiple planets:
  • • XO-2Nb - Hot Jupiter orbiting XO-2N
  • • XO-2Sb - Saturn-mass planet orbiting XO-2S
  • • Additional planets discovered in both systems
  • 6 Lyncis b: Super-Jupiter orbiting an orange giant
  • HD 75898 b: Jupiter-mass planet in eccentric orbit
  • HD 77912 b: Massive planet orbiting a giant star
