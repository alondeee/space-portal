    • Vulpecula — Foundational Data

  • Rank: 55th largest constellation
  • Area: 268 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: α Vul (Anser) — magnitude 4.44
  • Best Visible: June — September (Summer in Northern Hemisphere)
  • Mythology: Created in the 17th century by Johannes Hevelius. Originally named "Vulpecula cum Ansere" (the little fox with the goose). The goose (Anser) is now represented by the brightest star, α Vul.
  • Contains nearby star‑forming regions (Dumbbell Nebula).
  • Neighbours: Sagitta, Lyra, Pegasus, Equuleus, Delphinus, Aquila
  • Right Ascension (RA): 20h 00m to 21h 30m
  • Declination (Dec): +19° to +29°
