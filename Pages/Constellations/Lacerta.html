    • Lacerta — Foundational Data

  • Rank: 68th largest constellation
  • Area: 201 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: α Lacertae (mag 3.76)
  • Best Visible: September — November
  • Mythology: Introduced by Hevelius in 1687; not associated with classical myth.
  • Lies in the Milky Way, rich in star clusters.
  • Neighbours: Andromeda, Cassiopeia, Cepheus, Cygnus, Pegasus
