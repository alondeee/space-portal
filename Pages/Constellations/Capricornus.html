Capricornus

Capricornus, the sea-goat, is one of the faintest zodiacal constellations, with a mythical creature that has the front of a goat and the tail of a fish.

    • Capricornus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 40th largest Constellation
  • Area: 414 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Deneb Algedi (δ Capricorni) - magnitude 2.85
  • Best Visible: July - October
  • Mythology: Associated with the Sumerian god Enki and later the Greek god Pan, who transformed himself into a fish-goat to escape the monster Typhon.
  • The planet Neptune was discovered in this constellation by Johann Galle in 1846.
  • Neighbours: Aquarius, Aquila, Sagittarius, Microscopium, Piscis Austrinus
