Pictor

  • Major Stars:
  • 1. Alpha Pictoris - White main sequence star, brightest
  • 2. Beta Pictoris - Young star with debris disk and exoplanets
  • 3. Gamma Pictoris - Orange giant
  • 4. Delta Pictoris - Binary star system
  • 5. Eta Pictoris - Binary star
  • 6. Zeta Pictoris - Yellow-white dwarf
  • 7. Iota Pictoris - Binary star
  • 8. Kappa Pictoris - White main sequence
  • 9. Lambda Pictoris - Orange giant
  • 10. Mu Pictoris - Blue-white main sequence

    • Notable Stars:

  • Beta Pictoris - Famous for its debris disk and directly imaged exoplanet
  • AB Pictoris - Binary system with brown dwarf companion

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • NGC 1705 - Peculiar dwarf galaxy with starburst
  • Pictor A - Radio galaxy with relativistic jets
  • NGC 1803 - Barred spiral galaxy
  • ESO 121-6 - Irregular galaxy
  • PGC 16628 - Interacting galaxies
  • SN 2003hw - Type Ia supernova in Pictor A

    • Pictor - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 59th largest Constellation
  • Area: 247 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpha Pictoris (magnitude 3.24)
  • Best Visible: January (from southern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Created by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille as Equuleus Pictoris, representing an artist's easel.
  • Beta Pictoris is one of the most studied stars for its protoplanetary disk and directly imaged exoplanet.
  • Neighbours: Columba, Caelum, Dorado, Volans, Carina, Puppis
