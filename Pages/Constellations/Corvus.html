    • Corvus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 70th largest Constellation
  • Area: 184 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Gienah (γ Corvi)
  • Best Visible: March - June
  • Mythology: Represents the crow (or raven) in Greek mythology, servant of Apollo, punished for bringing bad news.
  • Four brightest stars form a distinctive asterism called "Spica's Spanker" or the "Sails".
  • Contains the famous Antennae Galaxies, currently in the process of colliding.
  • Neighbours: Virgo, Crater, Hydra
