Circinus

A small, faint constellation in the southern sky, representing a draftsman's compass

Circinus

  • Major Stars:
  • 1. Alpha Circini - Rapidly oscillating Ap star, brightest star
  • 2. Beta Circini - White main sequence star
  • 3. Gamma Circini - Binary star system
  • 4. Delta Circini - Multiple star system
  • 5. Epsilon Circini - Orange giant
  • 6. Zeta Circini - Blue-white main sequence star
  • 7. Theta Circini - Binary star
  • 8. AX Circini - Cepheid variable star
  • 9. BP Circini - Be star with circumstellar disk
  • 10. HD 129092 - Blue giant

    • Notable Stars:

  • Circinus X-1 - X-ray binary system with a neutron star
  • PSR B1509-58 - Pulsar creating the "Hand of God" nebula

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • Circinus Galaxy (ESO 97-G13) - Seyfert galaxy, closest to Milky Way
  • NGC 5823 - Open cluster
  • NGC 5715 - Open cluster
  • RCW 86 - Ancient supernova remnant
  • Circinus Molecular Cloud - Star-forming region
  • SN 185 - Possible historical supernova

    • Circinus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 85th largest Constellation
  • Area: 93 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpha Circini (magnitude 3.19)
  • Best Visible: July (from southern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Created by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille in the 18th century, representing the compass for drawing circles.
  • Contains the Circinus Galaxy, one of the closest Seyfert galaxies to the Milky Way.
  • Neighbours: Centaurus, Musca, Apus, Triangulum Australe, Norma, Lupus
