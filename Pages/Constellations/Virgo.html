Virgo

Virgo, the virgin, is the second largest constellation and contains the closest large galaxy cluster to us.

    • Notable Stars:

  • 70 Virginis - star with a confirmed exoplanet
  • Chi Virginis - one of the first stars found to have an excess of dust (debris disk)

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • Virgo Cluster of Galaxies - containing thousands of galaxies
  • M49, M58, M59, M60, M61, M84, M85, M86, M87, M89, M90, M98, M99, M100, M104 (Sombrero Galaxy)
  • M87 - Giant Elliptical Galaxy with a supermassive black hole (first ever imaged)
  • M104 (Sombrero Galaxy) - Spiral Galaxy
  • 3C 273 - Bright Quasar

    • Virgo - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 2nd largest Constellation
  • Area: 1294 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Spica (α Virginis) - 15th brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: March - July
  • Mythology: Associated with several goddesses including Demeter, Persephone, and Astraea, representing justice and harvest.
  • The constellation is rich in galaxies because it contains the Virgo Cluster, the nearest large galaxy cluster to the Milky Way.
  • Neighbours: Boötes, Coma Berenices, Leo, Crater, Corvus, Hydra, Libra, Serpens Caput
