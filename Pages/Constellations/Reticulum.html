  • 1. Alpha Reticuli - Yellow giant, brightest star
  • 2. Beta Reticuli - Binary star system
  • 3. Gamma Reticuli - Red giant
  • 4. Delta Reticuli - Binary star with exoplanet
  • 5. Epsilon Reticuli - Binary star with exoplanet
  • 6. Kappa Reticuli - Binary star
  • 7. Iota Reticuli - Orange giant
  • 8. Zeta Reticuli - Binary system, famous in ufology
  • 9. Eta Reticuli - White main sequence
  • 10. Theta Reticuli - Binary star

    • Notable Stars:

  • Zeta Reticuli - Binary system, prominent in UFO lore (Betty and Barney Hill)

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • NGC 1313 - Barred spiral galaxy, "Topsy-Turvy Galaxy"
  • NGC 1559 - Barred spiral galaxy with supernovae
  • PGC 13809 - Spiral galaxy
  • ESO 155-31 - Irregular galaxy
  • SN 2005df - Type Ia supernova in NGC 1559

    • Reticulum - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 82nd largest Constellation
  • Area: 114 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpha Reticuli (magnitude 3.33)
  • Best Visible: December - January (from southern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Named by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille to honor the reticle, an eyepiece used for precise measurements.
  • Zeta Reticuli is a famous binary system in ufology due to the Betty and Barney Hill abduction claim.
  • Neighbours: Horologium, Dorado, Hydrus
