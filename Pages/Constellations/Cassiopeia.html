    • Cassiopeia - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 25th largest Constellation
  • Area: 598 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Schedar (α Cassiopeiae)
  • Best Visible: November - February
  • Mythology: Represents the vain queen Cassiopeia, mother of Andromeda, who boasted about her beauty.
  • Circumpolar constellation for observers north of 40°N.
  • Contains many open clusters due to its location in the Milky Way.
  • Neighbours: Cepheus, Camelopardalis, Perseus, Andromeda, Lacerta
