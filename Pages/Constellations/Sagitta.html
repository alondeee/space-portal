Sagitta

Sagitta, the Arrow, is the third smallest constellation, shaped like its namesake.

    • Sagitta - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 86th largest Constellation
  • Area: 80 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: γ Sagittae - magnitude 3.47
  • Best Visible: July - September
  • Mythology: Often identified as the arrow shot by Hercules towards the Stymphalian Birds (Aquila) or the arrow used by Apollo to avenge Asclepius.
  • It is one of the 48 constellations listed by Ptolemy, despite its small size.
  • Neighbours: Vulpecula, Hercules, Aquila, Delphinus
