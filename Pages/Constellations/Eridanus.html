Eridanus

Eridanus is a long, winding constellation representing a river, flowing from the foot of Orion.

    • Notable Stars:

  • ε Eridani - nearby Sun-like star with a debris disk and known exoplanet
  • 40 Eridani (ο² Eridani) - triple star system with a white dwarf

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • IC 2118 (Witch Head Nebula)
  • NGC 1232 - Spiral Galaxy
  • NGC 1300 - Barred Spiral Galaxy
  • NGC 1535 - Planetary Nebula
  • Eridanus Group (Fornax Cluster) - Galaxy Cluster

    • Eridanus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 6th largest Constellation
  • Area: 1138 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Achernar (α Eridani) - 9th brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: November - January
  • Mythology: Represents the mythical river into which Phaëthon fell after losing control of his father Helios' sun chariot.
  • Achernar is one of the flattest stars known, due to its extremely fast rotation.
  • Neighbours: Cetus, Fornax, Phoenix, Hydrus, Tucana, Horologium, Caelum, Lepus, Orion, Taurus
