    • Pyxis - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 65th largest Constellation
  • Area: 221 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpha Pyxidis (magnitude 3.68)
  • Best Visible: March (from southern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Created by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille as Pyxis Nautica, representing a mariner's compass.
  • T Pyxidis is one of only 10 known recurrent novae in our galaxy.
  • Neighbours: Hydra, Antlia, Vela, Puppis
