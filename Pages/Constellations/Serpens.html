Serpens

Serpens, the serpent, is unique as it is divided into two parts: Serpens Caput (head) and Serpens Cauda (tail), separated by Ophiuchus.

    • Serpens - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 23rd largest (combined)
  • Area: 637 square degrees (Caput + Cauda)
  • Brightest Star: Unukalhai (α Serpentis) - magnitude 2.63
  • Best Visible: April - August
  • Mythology: Represents a serpent held by the serpent-bearer Ophiuchus. It is the only constellation split into two separate sections.
  • The Eagle Nebula (M16) is famously home to the "Pillars of Creation".
  • Neighbours: (Caput) Boötes, Virgo, Libra, Ophiuchus, Hercules; (Cauda) Ophiuchus, Sagittarius, Scutum, Aquila
