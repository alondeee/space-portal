    • Triangulum Australe — Foundational Data

  • Rank: 83rd largest constellation
  • Area: 110 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Atria (α TrA) - magnitude 1.91
  • Best Visible: June — August
  • Mythology: Named by Dutch explorers in the 16th century; represents the triangle used in navigation and surveying.
  • Forms a prominent triangle shape with three bright stars: Atria, β, and γ.
  • Located near the Milky Way, rich in star clusters.
  • Neighbours: Norma, Ara, Circinus, Apus, Lupus (corner)
