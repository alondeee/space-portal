    • Crater - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 53rd largest Constellation
  • Area: 282 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alkes (α Crateris)
  • Best Visible: March - June
  • Mythology: Represents the cup of Apollo, associated with the story of the crow (Corvus) who was sent to fetch water.
  • Name means "cup" in Latin, and its shape resembles a two-handled cup.
  • Contains the quasar RX J1131-1231, one of the most distant objects visible.
  • Neighbours: Virgo, Corvus, Hydra, Leo, Sextans
