Sagittarius

Sagittarius, the archer, is a zodiacal constellation that marks the direction of the center of our Milky Way galaxy.

    • Sagittarius - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 15th largest Constellation
  • Area: 867 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Kaus Australis (ε Sagittarii) - magnitude 1.79
  • Best Visible: June - September
  • Mythology: Represents a centaur archer, often identified with Crotus, a satyr companion of the Muses.
  • The constellation's brightest stars form an asterism known as the "Teapot". The Milky Way's center lies near the spout.
  • Neighbours: Aquila, Scutum, Serpens Cauda, Ophiuchus, Scorpius, Corona Australis, Telescopium, Indus, Microscopium, Capricornus
