    • Octans - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 50th largest Constellation
  • Area: 291 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Nu Octantis (magnitude 3.73)
  • Best Visible: Year-round (circumpolar in southern hemisphere)
  • Mythology: Named by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille to honor the octant, a navigational instrument.
  • Contains the south celestial pole, marked approximately by Sigma Octantis (Polaris Australis).
  • Neighbours: Tucana, Indus, Pavo, Apus, Chamaeleon, Mensa, Hydrus
