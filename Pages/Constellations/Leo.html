Leo

Leo, the lion, is a prominent zodiacal constellation with a distinctive sickle-shaped asterism marking the lion's head.

Leo

  • 1. Regulus (α Leonis)
  • 2. Denebola (β Leonis)
  • 3. Algieba (γ Leonis)
  • 4. Zosma (δ Leonis)
  • 5. Chort (θ Leonis)
  • 6. Algenubi (ε Leonis)
  • 7. Adhafera (ζ Leonis)
  • 8. Rasalas (μ Leonis)
  • 9. λ Leonis (Alterf)
  • 10. η Leonis

    • Notable Stars:

  • Wolf 359 - one of the closest stars to the Solar System
  • Gliese 436 - red dwarf with a Neptune-like exoplanet

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • M65, M66, NGC 3628 (Leo Triplet) - Interacting Galaxies
  • M95, M96, M105 - Spiral and Elliptical Galaxies (Leo I Group)
  • NGC 2903 - Barred Spiral Galaxy
  • Leo Ring - Giant primordial gas cloud
  • Leo I and Leo II - Dwarf Spheroidal Galaxies

    • Leo - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 12th largest Constellation
  • Area: 947 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Regulus (α Leonis) - 21st brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: February - May
  • Mythology: Represents the Nemean Lion from Greek mythology, slain by Heracles (Hercules) as the first of his twelve labours.
  • Regulus lies very close to the ecliptic and is often occulted by the Moon and sometimes by planets.
  • Neighbours: Ursa Major, Leo Minor, Lynx, Cancer, Hydra, Sextans, Crater, Virgo, Coma Berenices
