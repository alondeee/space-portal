    • Horologium - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 58th largest Constellation
  • Area: 249 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpha Horologii (magnitude 3.85)
  • Best Visible: December (from southern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Named by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille as Horologium Oscillitorium, honoring Christiaan Huygens' pendulum clock.
  • Contains part of the Horologium Supercluster, one of the largest known structures in the universe.
  • Neighbours: Eridanus, Caelum, Dorado, Reticulum, Hydrus
