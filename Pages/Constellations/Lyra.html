Lyra

Lyra, the harp, is a small but prominent constellation home to Vega, one of the brightest stars.

    • Notable Stars:

  • ε Lyrae - famous quadruple star system (Double Double)
  • RR Lyrae - prototype of RR Lyrae variable stars

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • M57 (Ring Nebula) - Planetary Nebula
  • M56 - Globular Cluster
  • NGC 6791 - Open Cluster
  • NGC 6745 - Interacting Galaxies (Bird's Head Galaxy)
  • KIC 8462852 (Tabby's Star) - star with unusual dimming events

    • Lyra - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 52nd largest Constellation
  • Area: 286 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Vega (α Lyrae) - 5th brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: June - September
  • Mythology: Represents the lyre of Orpheus, the legendary musician, placed in the sky after his death.
  • Vega was the northern pole star around 12,000 BC and will be again around 13,700 AD.
  • Neighbours: Draco, Hercules, Cygnus, Vulpecula
