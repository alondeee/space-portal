    • Hydrus — Foundational Data

  • Rank: 61st largest constellation
  • Area: 243 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: β Hydri (mag 2.80)
  • Best Visible: October — December
  • Mythology: Created by Plancius from observations of Keyzer & de Houtman.
  • β Hydri is one of the most Sun‑like stars in the sky.
  • Neighbours: Dorado, Eridanus, Horologium, Mensa, Octans, Phoenix, Reticulum, Tucana
