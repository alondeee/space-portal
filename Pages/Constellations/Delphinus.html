    • Delphinus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 69th largest Constellation
  • Area: 189 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Rotanev (β Delphini)
  • Best Visible: July - October
  • Mythology: Represents the dolphin that helped the sea god Poseidon win the heart of Amphitrite, or that saved the poet Arion.
  • Alpha and Beta Delphini have the backwards names "Sualocin" and "Rotanev" (Nicolaus Venator spelled backwards).
  • One of the smallest constellations but easily recognizable due to its compact shape.
  • Neighbours: Vulpecula, Sagitta, Aquila, Aquarius, Equuleus, Pegasus
