Puppis

Puppis, the stern of the ship, is a southern constellation once part of the vast Argo Navis, rich in Milky Way star fields.

    • Puppis - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 20th largest Constellation
  • Area: 673 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Naos (ζ Puppis) - magnitude 2.25
  • Best Visible: December - March (Southern Hemisphere)
  • Mythology: Represents the stern of the ship Argo from Greek mythology.
  • Naos means "ship" in Greek, and this star is one of the few O-type stars visible without a telescope.
  • Neighbours: Monoceros, Pyxis, Vela, Carina, Pictor, Columba, Canis Major, Hydra
