Orion

Orion, the hunter, is one of the most recognizable and prominent constellations in the night sky.

    • Orion - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 26th largest Constellation
  • Area: 594 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Rigel (β Orionis) - 7th brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: December - March
  • Mythology: Represents the mythical hunter Orion, who is accompanied by his two dogs (Canis Major and Canis Minor) and pursues the bull (Taurus).
  • Contains two of the ten brightest stars in the sky: Rigel and Betelgeuse.
  • Neighbours: Gemini, Taurus, Eridanus, Lepus, Monoceros
