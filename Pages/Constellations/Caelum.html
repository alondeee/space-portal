    • Caelum - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 81st largest Constellation
  • Area: 125 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpha Caeli (magnitude 4.44)
  • Best Visible: January (from southern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Created by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille, named Caelum Scalptorium meaning "engraver's chisel".
  • One of the faintest constellations with no stars brighter than magnitude 4.0.
  • Neighbours: Columba, Eridanus, Horologium, Dorado, Pictor, Puppis, Lepus
