Coma Berenices

Coma Berenices, Berenice's Hair, is a faint constellation named after the hair of Queen Berenice II, containing a rich cluster of galaxies.

    • Coma Berenices - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 42nd largest Constellation
  • Area: 386 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: β Comae Berenices - magnitude 4.26
  • Best Visible: February - May
  • Mythology: Represents the hair of Queen Berenice II, who cut off her hair as a votive offering for her husband's safe return from war. The hair was placed in the sky by the gods.
  • It is the only constellation named after a historical person (a queen).
  • Neighbours: Canes Venatici, Ursa Major, Leo, Virgo, Boötes
