Cepheus

Cepheus is home to the prototype of important variable stars: Delta Cephei

Cepheus

  • Major Stars:
  • 1. Alderamin (α Cephei)
  • 2. Delta Cephei (δ Cephei) - prototype Cepheid variable
  • 3. Gamma Cephei (Errai)
  • 4. Beta Cephei (Alfirk)
  • 5. Zeta Cephei
  • 6. Eta Cephei
  • 7. Theta Cephei
  • 8. Mu Cephei (Herschel's Garnet Star)
  • 9. Xi Cephei
  • 10. Iota Cephei

    • Notable Stars:

  • Mu Cephei - red supergiant, one of largest stars visible
  • VV Cephei - eclipsing binary with huge red supergiant
  • Kruger 60 - binary star system

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • NGC 188 - one of oldest open clusters
  • IC 1396 - emission nebula with Elephant's Trunk Nebula
  • NGC 7023 - Iris Nebula
  • NGC 6946 - Fireworks Galaxy
  • NGC 7538 - emission nebula

    • Cepheus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 27th largest Constellation
  • Area: 588 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alderamin (α Cephei)
  • Best Visible: August - November
  • Mythology: Represents King Cepheus, husband of Cassiopeia and father of Andromeda in Greek mythology.
  • Delta Cephei is the prototype for Cepheid variable stars, crucial for measuring cosmic distances.
  • Contains some of the largest known stars (Mu Cephei, VV Cephei).
  • Neighbours: Cassiopeia, Camelopardalis, Draco, Cygnus, Lacerta, Ursa Minor
