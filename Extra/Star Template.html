📜 History · nomenclature · cultural significance

Discovery / earliest record: known since antiquity; named 'Rigel' from Arabic Rijl Jauzah "foot of the giant".

Catalog designations: β Orionis (Bayer), HD 34085, HIP 24436, HR 1713, SAO 131907.

Name origin / mythology: marks the foot of Orion (hunter). In Egyptian mythology, possibly associated with Sah.

Historical scientific milestones: first resolved as binary by W. Herschel (1782); used for spectroscopic parallax calibration.
