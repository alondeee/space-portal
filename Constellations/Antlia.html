    • Antlia - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 62nd largest Constellation
  • Area: 239 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpha Antliae (magnitude 4.25)
  • Best Visible: April (from southern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Named by Abbé Nicolas Louis de Lacaille in the 18th century, representing an air pump (Antlia Pneumatica).
  • Contains the Antlia Cluster, one of the richest galaxy clusters near the Milky Way.
  • Neighbours: Hydra, Pyxis, Vela, Centaurus
