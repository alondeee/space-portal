Ophiuchus

Ophiuchus, the serpent-bearer, is a large constellation straddling the celestial equator.

    • Ophiuchus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 11th largest Constellation
  • Area: 948 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Rasalhague (α Ophiuchi) - magnitude 2.07
  • Best Visible: May - August
  • Mythology: Depicts a man (often identified as Asclepius, the god of medicine) holding a serpent (the constellation Serpens).
  • The Sun passes through Ophiuchus from Nov 30 to Dec 18, making it an unofficial zodiacal constellation.
  • Neighbours: Hercules, Serpens Caput, Serpens Cauda, Scorpius, Sagittarius, Aquila
