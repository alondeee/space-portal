Canis Minor

The Lesser Dog - Home to Procyon, one of the brightest stars

Canis Minor - Major Stars

  • 1. Procyon (α Canis Minoris) - 8th brightest star, binary system
  • 2. Gomeisa (β Canis Minoris) - Hot blue-white star
  • 3. γ Canis Minoris
  • 4. δ¹ Canis Minoris
  • 5. δ² Canis Minoris
  • 6. δ³ Canis Minoris
  • 7. η Canis Minoris
  • 8. ζ Canis Minoris

Notable Stars & Exoplanets

  • HD 66141 - K-type giant with a planet
  • Luyten's Star - Nearby red dwarf

Deep Sky Objects

  • NGC 2485 - Spiral galaxy
  • NGC 2508 - Lenticular galaxy
  • NGC 2538 - Barred spiral galaxy

Canis Minor - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 71st largest constellation
  • Area: 183 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Procyon (α Canis Minoris) - 8th brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: Winter (December - March)
  • Mythology: Represents one of Orion's hunting dogs
  • Contains Procyon, part of the "Winter Triangle" with Sirius and Betelgeuse
  • Neighbours: Gemini, Cancer, Hydra, Monoceros
