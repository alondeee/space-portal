    • Cygnus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 16th largest Constellation
  • Area: 804 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Deneb (α Cygni)
  • Best Visible: July - October
  • Mythology: Represents the swan, associated with Zeus who transformed into a swan, or Orpheus.
  • The Northern Cross is a prominent asterism within Cygnus.
  • Cygnus X-1 was the first widely accepted black hole discovered (1971).
  • 61 Cygni was the first star (other than Sun) to have its distance measured (1838).
  • Neighbours: Cepheus, Draco, Lyra, Vulpecula, Pegasus, Lacerta
