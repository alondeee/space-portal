Sculptor

A southern constellation representing a sculptor's workshop

Sculptor

  • Major Stars:
  • 1. Alpha Sculptoris - Blue-white giant, brightest star
  • 2. Beta Sculptoris - Binary star system
  • 3. Gamma Sculptoris - Yellow giant
  • 4. Delta Sculptoris - Binary star
  • 5. Eta Sculptoris - Red giant
  • 6. Zeta Sculptoris - Binary star
  • 7. Iota Sculptoris - White main sequence
  • 8. Lambda Sculptoris - Binary star
  • 9. Mu Sculptoris - Orange giant
  • 10. Pi Sculptoris - Yellow-white dwarf

    • Notable Stars:

  • HD 4113 - Star with exoplanet and binary companion
  • WASP-45 - Hosts a hot Jupiter exoplanet

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • Sculptor Dwarf Galaxy - Dwarf spheroidal, Milky Way satellite
  • NGC 253 (Sculptor Galaxy) - Brightest galaxy in Sculptor Group
  • Sculptor Group - Galaxy group closest to Local Group
  • NGC 55 - Irregular galaxy, member of Sculptor Group
  • NGC 300 - Spiral galaxy, Sculptor Group member
  • NGC 7793 - Spiral galaxy in Sculptor Group
  • Cartwheel Galaxy - Ring galaxy, result of collision

    • Sculptor - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 36th largest Constellation
  • Area: 475 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpha Sculptoris (magnitude 4.30)
  • Best Visible: November - December (from southern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Created by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille as Apparatus Sculptoris, representing a sculptor's studio.
  • Contains the Sculptor Group, the nearest group of galaxies to the Local Group.
  • Neighbours: Cetus, Aquarius, Piscis Austrinus, Grus, Phoenix, Fornax
