Pegasus

Pegasus, the winged horse, is a large northern constellation famous for its Great Square asterism.

Pegasus

  • 1. Enif (ε Pegasi)
  • 2. Scheat (β Pegasi)
  • 3. Markab (α Pegasi)
  • 4. Algenib (γ Pegasi)
  • 5. Matar (η Pegasi)
  • 6. Baham (θ Pegasi)
  • 7. ι Pegasi
  • 8. λ Pegasi
  • 9. μ Pegasi (Sadalbari)
  • 10. ζ Pegasi (Homam)

    • Notable Stars:

  • 51 Pegasi - first Sun-like star found to have an exoplanet (51 Pegasi b / Dimidium)
  • IK Pegasi - nearest supernova candidate to Earth

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • M15 - Globular Cluster
  • NGC 7331 (Deer Lick Galaxy) - Spiral Galaxy
  • Stephan's Quintet - Compact Galaxy Group
  • Einstein's Cross (gravitationally lensed quasar)
  • NGC 1 - Spiral Galaxy
  • Pegasus Dwarf Spheroidal Galaxy - Local Group galaxy

    • Pegasus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 7th largest Constellation
  • Area: 1121 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Enif (ε Pegasi) - magnitude 2.38
  • Best Visible: August - November
  • Mythology: Represents the winged horse Pegasus, born from the blood of Medusa when she was slain by Perseus.
  • The Great Square of Pegasus is a key asterism for autumn navigation in the northern hemisphere.
  • Neighbours: Andromeda, Lacerta, Cygnus, Vulpecula, Delphinus, Equuleus, Aquarius, Pisces
