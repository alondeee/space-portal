    • Draco - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 8th largest Constellation
  • Area: 1083 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Eltanin (γ Draconis)
  • Best Visible: Year-round (circumpolar)
  • Mythology: Represents the dragon Ladon, who guarded the golden apples in the Garden of the Hesperides, slain by Hercules.
  • Thuban was the North Pole star around 3000 BCE, when the Egyptian pyramids were built.
  • Long, winding constellation that wraps around Ursa Minor.
  • Contains the Cat's Eye Nebula, one of the most complex planetary nebulae known.
  • Neighbours: Boötes, Hercules, Lyra, Cygnus, Cepheus, Ursa Major, Ursa Minor
