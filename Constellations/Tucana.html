    • Tucana — Foundational Data

  • Rank: 48th largest constellation
  • Area: 295 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: α Tuc (mag 2.86)
  • Best Visible: August — November
  • Mythology: Named by Dutch explorers; commemorates the toucan bird.
  • Contains 47 Tuc, one of the brightest globular clusters in the sky.
  • Neighbours: Grus, Indus, Octans, Hydrus, Eridanus, Phoenix
