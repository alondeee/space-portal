Lupus

Lupus, the wolf, is a constellation located in the southern sky, nestled between Centaurus and Scorpius.

    • Lupus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 46th largest Constellation
  • Area: 334 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: α Lupi - magnitude 2.30
  • Best Visible: April - July
  • Mythology: In ancient times, it was an asterism within Centaurus, later becoming the separate figure of a wild beast, often identified as a wolf.
  • The constellation is crossed by the Milky Way, making it rich in deep-sky objects.
  • Neighbours: Norma, Circinus, Centaurus, Hydra, Libra, Scorpius
