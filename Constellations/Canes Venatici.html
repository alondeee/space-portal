Canes Venatici

The Hunting Dogs - Home to the Whirlpool Galaxy and many other deep sky treasures

Canes Venatici - Major Stars

  • 1. Cor Caroli (α Canum Venaticorum) - Double star, named "Heart of Charles"
  • 2. Chara (β Canum Venaticorum) - Yellow dwarf similar to Sun
  • 3. 24 Canum Venaticorum
  • 4. Y Canum Venaticorum (La Superba) - Carbon star, one of reddest stars
  • 5. AW Canum Venaticorum
  • 6. BH Canum Venaticorum
  • 7. 20 Canum Venaticorum
  • 8. 25 Canum Venaticorum

Notable Stars & Exoplanets

  • Chara (β CVn) - Solar analog, search target for extraterrestrial life
  • La Superba - One of the reddest and most luminous carbon stars
  • HAT-P-36 - Star with a transiting exoplanet

Deep Sky Objects

  • M51 (Whirlpool Galaxy) - Famous interacting spiral galaxy
  • M63 (Sunflower Galaxy) - Spiral galaxy
  • M94 - Spiral galaxy with starburst ring
  • M106 - Spiral galaxy with active nucleus
  • M3 - Globular cluster, one of the brightest
  • NGC 4244 - Edge-on spiral galaxy
  • NGC 4449 - Irregular starburst galaxy
  • NGC 4490 (Cocoon Galaxy) - Interacting galaxy pair

Canes Venatici - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 38th largest constellation
  • Area: 465 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Cor Caroli (α Canum Venaticorum)
  • Best Visible: Spring (March - June)
  • Mythology: Represents the hunting dogs of Boötes (Asterion and Chara)
  • Created by Johannes Hevelius in the 17th century
  • Exceptionally rich in galaxies despite its small size
  • Neighbours: Ursa Major, Boötes, Coma Berenices
