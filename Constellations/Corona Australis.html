    • Corona Australis - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 80th largest Constellation
  • Area: 128 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alphekka Meridiana (α Coronae Australis)
  • Best Visible: July - September
  • Mythology: Represents the crown of the southern hemisphere, often associated with the centaur or the god Dionysus.
  • Contains one of the nearest star-forming regions to Earth (Corona Australis Molecular Cloud).
  • Small but rich in nebulae despite its size.
  • Neighbours: Sagittarius, Scorpius, Ara, Telescopium
