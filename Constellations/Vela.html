Vela

Vela, the sails of the ship, is a southern constellation originally part of Argo Navis, containing the famous Vela Supernova Remnant.

Vela

  • 1. γ Velorum (Regor)
  • 2. δ Velorum (Alsephina)
  • 3. κ Velorum
  • 4. λ Velorum (Suhail)
  • 5. μ Velorum
  • 6. φ Velorum
  • 7. ψ Velorum
  • 8. b Velorum
  • 9. o Velorum
  • 10. N Velorum

    • Notable Stars:

  • γ Velorum - multiple star system containing the brightest Wolf-Rayet star in the sky
  • δ Velorum - bright triple star system, part of the "False Cross" asterism

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • Vela Supernova Remnant - Remnant of a supernova that exploded ~11,000 years ago
  • Vela Pulsar - Neutron star in the remnant
  • Gum Nebula - Large emission nebula (shared with Puppis)
  • NGC 3132 (Eight-Burst Nebula) - Planetary Nebula
  • NGC 2736 (Pencil Nebula) - Part of Vela SNR
  • RCW 38 - Star-forming region

    • Vela - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 32nd largest Constellation
  • Area: 500 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: γ Velorum (Regor) - magnitude 1.75 (combined)
  • Best Visible: January - April (Southern Hemisphere)
  • Mythology: Represents the sails of the ship Argo from Greek mythology.
  • The constellation contains the Vela Pulsar, which was the first pulsar detected in visible light.
  • Neighbours: Pyxis, Puppis, Carina, Centaurus, Antlia
