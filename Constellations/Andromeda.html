    • Andromeda - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 19th largest Constellation
  • Area: 722 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpheratz (α And) - also part of the Great Square of Pegasus
  • Best Visible: November - December
  • Mythology: Princess Andromeda, chained to a rock as a sacrifice to Cetus
  • Neighbours: Perseus, Cassiopeia, Lacerta, Pegasus, Pisces, Triangulum
  • Home to the nearest major galaxy to the Milky Way (M31)
  • Part of the Perseus Family of constellations
