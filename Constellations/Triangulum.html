Triangulum

Triangulum is a small, ancient constellation shaped like a triangle, home to a famous nearby galaxy.

    • Notable Stars:

  • α Trianguli - a spectroscopic binary and one of the first stars to have its parallax measured
  • R Trianguli - a Mira-type variable star

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • M33 (Triangulum Galaxy) - Spiral Galaxy, member of the Local Group
  • NGC 604 - Giant H II region within M33
  • NGC 925 - Barred Spiral Galaxy
  • NGC 672 - Barred Spiral Galaxy

    • Triangulum - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 78th largest Constellation
  • Area: 132 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: β Trianguli - magnitude 3.00
  • Best Visible: October - December
  • Mythology: In ancient Greece, it was known as Deltoton, representing the Nile Delta or the island of Sicily, offered to the gods by Ceres.
  • The Triangulum Galaxy (M33) is the third largest member of the Local Group, after Andromeda and the Milky Way.
  • Neighbours: Andromeda, Perseus, Aries, Pisces
