Argo Navis

Argo Navis is now divided into three modern constellations

Argo Navis (The Ship Argo)

Originally the largest constellation, it was split by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille in 1763 into Carina, Puppis, and Vela. Pyxis (the compass) was also carved out nearby.

  • 1. Canopus (α Carinae) - brightest in Carina
  • 2. Naos (ζ Puppis) - brightest in Puppis
  • 3. γ Velorum (Regor) - brightest in Vela
  • 4. Miaplacidus (β Carinae)
  • 5. Avior (ε Carinae)
  • 6. δ Velorum (Koo She)
  • 7. λ Velorum (Suhail)
  • 8. π Puppis (Ahadi)
  • 9. τ Puppis
  • 10. Aspidiske (ι Carinae)

    • Argo Navis - Foundational Data

  • Original Rank: 1st (largest constellation)
  • Original Area: > 1800 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Canopus (α Carinae) - 2nd brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: February - March
  • Mythology: Represents the ship Argo used by Jason and the Argonauts
  • Modern Constituents: Carina (Keel), Puppis (Stern), Vela (Sails)
  • Bayer Designations: Johann Bayer assigned 60 stars (α to ω) across the whole ship
  • Milky Way: The galactic plane runs through it, making it rich in clusters and nebulae
