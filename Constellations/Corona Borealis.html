    • Corona Borealis - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 73rd largest Constellation
  • Area: 179 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alphecca (α Coronae Borealis)
  • Best Visible: May - August
  • Mythology: Represents the crown given by Dionysus to Ariadne, daughter of King Minos of Crete.
  • Distinctive semicircular shape makes it one of the easiest constellations to identify.
  • Contains the prototype of a rare class of variable stars (R Coronae Borealis).
  • Neighbours: Hercules, Boötes, Serpens Caput
