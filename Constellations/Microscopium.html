    • Microscopium - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 66th largest Constellation
  • Area: 210 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Gamma Microscopii (magnitude 4.67)
  • Best Visible: September (from southern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Created by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille in the 18th century, representing the microscope, a scientific instrument of his era.
  • AU Microscopii is one of the nearest young stellar systems with a debris disk and multiple planets.
  • Neighbours: Capricornus, Sagittarius, Indus, Grus, Piscis Austrinus
