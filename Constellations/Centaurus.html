    • Centaurus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 9th largest Constellation
  • Area: 1060 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpha Centauri (Rigil Kentaurus) - 3rd brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: March - June
  • Mythology: Represents the centaur, a half-human, half-horse creature from Greek mythology, often identified with Chiron.
  • Contains the closest star system to Earth (Alpha Centauri).
  • Home to the largest and brightest globular cluster (Omega Centauri).
  • Neighbours: Hydra, Lupus, Circinus, Crux, Musca, Antlia, Vela, Carina
