Carina

Carina, the keel of the ship, is part of the former giant constellation Argo Navis and contains some of the brightest stars and most spectacular nebulae.

    • Notable Stars:

  • η Carinae - extremely luminous hypergiant star, highly variable, in the process of dying
  • V382 Carinae (x Carinae) - yellow hypergiant star

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • η Carinae Nebula (NGC 3372) - One of the largest and brightest nebulae
  • Homunculus Nebula - Bipolar nebula surrounding η Carinae
  • Keyhole Nebula - Dark nebula within the Carina Nebula
  • NGC 2516 - Open Cluster
  • IC 2602 (Southern Pleiades) - Open Cluster
  • NGC 2808 - Globular Cluster
  • NGC 3132 (Eight-Burst Nebula) - Planetary Nebula

    • Carina - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 34th largest Constellation
  • Area: 494 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Canopus (α Carinae) - 2nd brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: January - April (Southern Hemisphere)
  • Mythology: Represents the keel of the ship Argo, used by Jason and the Argonauts.
  • Canopus is a white giant star and is used for spacecraft navigation due to its brightness and position.
  • Neighbours: Vela, Puppis, Pictor, Volans, Chamaeleon, Musca, Centaurus
