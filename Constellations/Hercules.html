Hercules

Hercules is a large constellation named after the Roman hero, known for its keystone asterism.

    • Hercules - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 5th largest Constellation
  • Area: 1225 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Kornephoros (β Herculis) - magnitude 2.77
  • Best Visible: April - July
  • Mythology: Represents the legendary hero Herakles, son of Zeus, known for his twelve labours.
  • Contains the solar apex, the direction the Sun is moving relative to local stars.
  • Neighbours: Draco, Boötes, Corona Borealis, Serpens Caput, Ophiuchus, Aquila, Sagitta, Vulpecula, Lyra
