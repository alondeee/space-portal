    • Aquila - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 22nd largest Constellation
  • Area: 652 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Altair (α Aquilae) - 12th brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: August - September
  • Mythology: Represents the eagle that carried Zeus' thunderbolts
  • Location: Crosses the celestial equator
  • Neighbours: Hercules, Ophiuchus, Serpens Cauda, Scutum, Sagittarius, Capricornus, Aquarius, Delphinus, Sagitta
  • Altair is one of the vertices of the Summer Triangle (with Vega and Deneb)
