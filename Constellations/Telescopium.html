A southern constellation representing a telescope

Telescopium

  • Major Stars:
  • 1. Alpha Telescopii - Blue-white giant, brightest star
  • 2. Zeta Telescopii - Yellow giant
  • 3. Epsilon Telescopii - Binary star system
  • 4. Lambda Telescopii - Binary star
  • 5. Iota Telescopii - Orange giant
  • 6. Delta Telescopii - Binary star
  • 7. Eta Telescopii - White main sequence star
  • 8. Rho Telescopii - Yellow-white dwarf
  • 9. Mu Telescopii - Red giant
  • 10. Kappa Telescopii - Binary star

    • Notable Stars:

  • HD 191760 - Star with exoplanet discovered in 2011
  • RR Telescopii - Symbiotic nova, erupted in 1944

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • NGC 6584 - Globular cluster
  • NGC 6861 - Lenticular galaxy
  • NGC 6845 - Interacting galaxy group ("Telescopium Group")
  • IC 4699 - Barred spiral galaxy
  • IC 4889 - Elliptical galaxy
  • ESO 184-54 - Irregular galaxy

    • Telescopium - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 57th largest Constellation
  • Area: 252 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpha Telescopii (magnitude 3.49)
  • Best Visible: August (from southern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Created by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille to honor the telescope, a key scientific instrument.
  • RR Telescopii is a famous symbiotic nova that brightened dramatically in the 1940s.
  • Neighbours: Ara, Corona Australis, Pavo, Indus, Microscopium, Sagittarius
