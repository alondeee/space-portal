    • Indus — Foundational Data

  • Rank: 49th largest constellation
  • Area: 294 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: α Ind (mag 3.11)
  • Best Visible: August — October
  • Mythology: Named by Dutch explorers to honor the people of the Americas.
  • ε Indi is a prime target for exoplanet searches.
  • Neighbours: Microscopium, Sagittarius, Telescopium, Pavo, Octans, Tucana, Grus
