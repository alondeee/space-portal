Camelopardalis

The Giraffe - A large but faint northern constellation

Camelopardalis - Major Stars

  • 1. β Camelopardalis - Binary star, brightest in constellation
  • 2. α Camelopardalis - Blue-white supergiant
  • 3. 7 Camelopardalis
  • 4. γ Camelopardalis
  • 5. HD 42818 (CS Camelopardalis)
  • 6. BE Camelopardalis
  • 7. HD 33564 - Star with an exoplanet
  • 8. HD 104985 (Tonatiuh) - Giant star with planet

Notable Stars & Exoplanets

  • HD 33564 - Has a massive exoplanet
  • HD 104985 (Tonatiuh) - Yellow giant with planet
  • Mira variables including T Camelopardalis

Deep Sky Objects

  • NGC 2403 - Spiral galaxy, member of M81 group
  • IC 342 (Caldwell 5) - Hidden galaxy obscured by Milky Way
  • NGC 1569 - Dwarf irregular galaxy with starburst activity
  • NGC 1502 - Open cluster
  • Kemble's Cascade - Asterism of stars
  • NGC 2655 - Lenticular galaxy

Camelopardalis - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 18th largest constellation
  • Area: 757 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: β Camelopardalis
  • Best Visible: February - May (Circumpolar)
  • Mythology: Created by Petrus Plancius to represent the animal that carried Rebecca to Isaac
  • Circumpolar constellation for many northern latitudes
  • Contains Kemble's Cascade, a beautiful asterism for binoculars
  • Neighbours: Draco, Ursa Major, Lynx, Auriga, Perseus, Cassiopeia, Cepheus
