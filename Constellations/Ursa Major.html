Ursa Major

Ursa Major, the Great Bear, contains the famous Big Dipper asterism, one of the most recognizable patterns in the sky.

Ursa Major

  • Major Stars (The Big Dipper):
  • 1. Alioth (ε Ursae Majoris)
  • 2. Dubhe (α Ursae Majoris)
  • 3. Merak (β Ursae Majoris)
  • 4. Phecda (γ Ursae Majoris)
  • 5. Megrez (δ Ursae Majoris)
  • 6. Mizar (ζ Ursae Majoris) & Alcor (80 UMa)
  • 7. Alkaid (η Ursae Majoris)
  • Other Major Stars:
  • 8. Talitha (ι Ursae Majoris)
  • 9. Tania Borealis (λ UMa) & Tania Australis (μ UMa)
  • 10. Alula Borealis (ν UMa) & Alula Australis (ξ UMa)

    • Ursa Major - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 3rd largest Constellation
  • Area: 1280 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alioth (ε Ursae Majoris) - magnitude 1.76
  • Best Visible: February - May (circumpolar for much of northern hemisphere)
  • Mythology: Represents Callisto, a nymph transformed into a bear by Zeus' wife Hera, and later placed in the sky by Zeus.
  • The stars Dubhe and Merak (The Pointers) point the way to Polaris, the North Star.
  • Neighbours: Draco, Camelopardalis, Lynx, Leo Minor, Leo, Coma Berenices, Canes Venatici, Boötes
