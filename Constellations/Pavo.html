    • Pavo — Foundational Data

  • Rank: 44th largest constellation
  • Area: 378 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: α Pav (Peacock, mag 1.94)
  • Best Visible: July — September
  • Mythology: Named by Dutch explorers after the peacock, bird of Hera.
  • Contains NGC 6752, one of the finest globular clusters.
  • Neighbours: Telescopium, Ara, Apus, Octans, Indus
