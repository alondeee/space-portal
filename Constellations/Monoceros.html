    • Monoceros — Foundational Data

  • Rank: 35th largest constellation
  • Area: 482 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: α Monocerotis (mag 3.93)
  • Best Visible: January — March
  • Mythology: Introduced by Plancius in 17th century; celestial unicorn.
  • Rich in nebulae and clusters due to Milky Way position.
  • Neighbours: Canis Major, Canis Minor, Gemini, Hydra, Lepus, Orion, Puppis
