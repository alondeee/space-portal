Piscis Austrinus

Piscis Austrinus, the Southern Fish, is a small constellation known for its bright star Fomalhaut.

    • Notable Stars:

  • Fomalhaut - one of the brightest stars, with a circumstellar disk and candidate exoplanet (Fomalhaut b, which may be a dust cloud)
  • TW Piscis Austrini - a young star associated with the Fomalhaut system

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • NGC 7314 - Spiral Galaxy
  • NGC 7135 - Lenticular Galaxy
  • NGC 7172 - Spiral Galaxy
  • Fomalhaut's Debris Disk - one of the first directly imaged extrasolar systems

    • Piscis Austrinus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 60th largest Constellation
  • Area: 245 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Fomalhaut (α Piscis Austrini) - 18th brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: August - October
  • Mythology: Represents the parent of the two fishes of the zodiac constellation Pisces. It is often shown drinking the water poured by Aquarius.
  • Fomalhaut was considered one of the four "Royal Stars" of ancient Persia, marking the winter solstice.
  • Neighbours: Aquarius, Capricornus, Microscopium, Grus, Sculptor
