Cetus

Cetus is the sea monster constellation, home to the famous variable star Mira

Cetus

  • 1. Deneb Kaitos (β Ceti)
  • 2. Mira (ο Ceti) - first discovered variable star
  • 3. Diphda (β Ceti) - brightest star
  • 4. Menkar (α Ceti)
  • 5. Gamma Ceti
  • 6. Tau Ceti - Sun-like star
  • 7. Eta Ceti
  • 8. Zeta Ceti
  • 9. Iota Ceti
  • 10. Theta Ceti

    • Notable Stars:

  • Tau Ceti - one of nearest Sun-like stars, has exoplanets
  • UV Ceti (Luyten's Star) - prototype flare star
  • 94 Ceti - has an exoplanet

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • M77 (NGC 1068) - Seyfert galaxy
  • NGC 1055 - spiral galaxy
  • NGC 247 - Cetus Dwarf Galaxy
  • NGC 246 (Skull Nebula) - planetary nebula
  • IC 1613 - dwarf irregular galaxy

    • Cetus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 4th largest Constellation
  • Area: 1231 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Deneb Kaitos (β Ceti) / Diphda
  • Best Visible: October - December
  • Mythology: Represents the sea monster sent by Poseidon to devour Andromeda, slain by Perseus.
  • Mira was the first variable star discovered (by David Fabricius in 1596).
  • Tau Ceti is one of the primary targets in the search for extraterrestrial intelligence (SETI).
  • Neighbours: Aries, Pisces, Aquarius, Sculptor, Fornax, Eridanus, Taurus
