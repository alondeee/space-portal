Perseus

Perseus is a northern constellation, rich in Milky Way star fields and home to the famous variable star Algol.

    • Notable Stars:

  • Algol (β Persei) - prototype of Algol variable eclipsing binary stars (the Demon Star)
  • GK Persei - Nova Persei 1901

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • M34 - Open Cluster
  • M76 (Little Dumbbell Nebula) - Planetary Nebula
  • NGC 869 and NGC 884 (Double Cluster) - Open Clusters (shared with Cassiopeia)
  • California Nebula (NGC 1499) - Emission Nebula
  • Perseus Cluster (Abell 426) - Galaxy Cluster
  • IC 348 - Star-forming region and open cluster

    • Perseus - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 24th largest Constellation
  • Area: 615 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Mirfak (α Persei) - magnitude 1.79
  • Best Visible: October - March
  • Mythology: Represents the Greek hero Perseus, who slew Medusa and rescued Andromeda from a sea monster.
  • Hosts the annual Perseid meteor shower, one of the most spectacular.
  • Neighbours: Cassiopeia, Andromeda, Triangulum, Aries, Taurus, Auriga, Camelopardalis
