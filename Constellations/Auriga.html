    • Auriga - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 21st largest Constellation
  • Area: 657 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Capella (α Aurigae) - 6th brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: December - February
  • Mythology: Represents a charioteer, often depicted holding a goat (Capella) and her kids.
  • Location: Northern celestial hemisphere (NQ2)
  • Neighbours: Camelopardalis, Perseus, Taurus, Gemini, Lynx
