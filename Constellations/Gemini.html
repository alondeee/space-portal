Gemini

Gemini, the twins, is a zodiacal constellation known for its two bright stars, Castor and Pollux.

  • Gemini Observatory - Named after the constellation

    • Gemini - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 30th largest Constellation
  • Area: 514 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Pollux (β Geminorum) - 17th brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: December - April
  • Mythology: Represents the twin brothers Castor and Pollux from Greek mythology, sons of Leda. Pollux was immortal, while Castor was mortal.
  • The constellation is associated with the annual Geminids meteor shower in December, one of the best of the year.
  • Neighbours: Lynx, Auriga, Taurus, Orion, Monoceros, Canis Minor, Cancer
