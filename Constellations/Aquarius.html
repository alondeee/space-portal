    • Aquarius - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 10th largest Constellation
  • Area: 980 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Sadalsuud (β Aquarii)
  • Best Visible: October - November
  • Zodiac Position: January 20 - February 18
  • Mythology: Associated with Ganymede, cupbearer to the gods
  • Neighbours: Pisces, Pegasus, Equuleus, Delphinus, Aquila, Capricornus, Piscis Austrinus, Sculptor, Cetus
  • Radiant of the Eta Aquariids and Delta Aquariids meteor showers
