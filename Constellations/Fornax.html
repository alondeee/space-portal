Fornax

A constellation in the southern sky, named for the chemical furnace

Fornax

  • Major Stars:
  • 1. Alpha Fornacis - Binary star system, brightest star
  • 2. Beta Fornacis - Yellow giant
  • 3. Nu Fornacis - White main sequence star
  • 4. Omega Fornacis - Binary star
  • 5. Delta Fornacis - Red giant
  • 6. Phi Fornacis - Orange giant with exoplanet
  • 7. Kappa Fornacis - Binary system
  • 8. Iota Fornacis - White dwarf star
  • 9. Gamma Fornacis - Yellow-white dwarf
  • 10. Eta Fornacis - Blue-white main sequence star

    • Notable Stars:

  • HD 20807 - Hosts an exoplanet discovered by radial velocity
  • WASP-72 - Hosts a hot Jupiter exoplanet

    • Famous Deepsky objects:

  • Fornax Cluster - Major galaxy cluster with ~58 galaxies
  • NGC 1316 (Fornax A) - Radio galaxy, brightest in cluster
  • NGC 1365 - Barred spiral galaxy, Great Barred Spiral
  • Fornax Dwarf - Dwarf spheroidal galaxy, Milky Way satellite
  • NGC 1399 - Elliptical galaxy
  • NGC 1404 - Elliptical galaxy
  • Hubble Ultra Deep Field - Located in Fornax

    • Fornax - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 41st largest Constellation
  • Area: 398 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Alpha Fornacis (magnitude 3.85)
  • Best Visible: December (from southern latitudes)
  • Mythology: Named by Nicolas Louis de Lacaille as Fornax Chemica, representing a chemical furnace.
  • Contains the Hubble Ultra Deep Field, one of the deepest images of the universe ever taken.
  • Neighbours: Cetus, Sculptor, Phoenix, Eridanus
