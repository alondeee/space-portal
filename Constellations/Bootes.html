    • Boötes - Foundational Data

  • Rank: 13th largest Constellation
  • Area: 907 square degrees
  • Brightest Star: Arcturus (α Boötis) - 4th brightest in night sky
  • Best Visible: April - July
  • Mythology: Represents the herdsman or plowman, often associated with Ursa Major.
  • Contains the "Bootes Void" - a massive, nearly empty region of space.
  • Neighbours: Draco, Hercules, Corona Borealis, Serpens, Virgo, Coma Berenices, Canes Venatici, Ursa Major
