Solar System Facts

Scale

The Sun contains 99.86% of all mass in the Solar System.

Temperatures

Venus is hotter than Mercury due to its thick atmosphere.

Rings

Saturn isn't the only planet with rings. Jupiter, Uranus, and Neptune also have ring systems.

Water

Earth is the only known planet with liquid water on its surface.

Orbit

Venus takes 243 days to complete one rotation on its axis while it takes 225 days to orbit the Sun.

Moons

Saturn has 293 moons, which is the highest in the Solar System. The largest moon is Titan.

Pluto

Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet in 2006 by the International Astronomical Union (IAU) and has a surface temperature of -387°F to -400°F, making it one of the coldest planets in the Solar System.

Water

Astronaut Neil Armstrong was the first person to step foot on the moon, along with Buzz Aldrin and Michael Collins on Apollo 11, July 20th, 1969. They landed on the Sea of Tranquility, or Mare Tranquillitatis.
